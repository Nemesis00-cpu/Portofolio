A little something about her

We create pieces for the woman she's becoming.

Thoughtfully designed with feminine details, timeless silhouettes, and a quiet sense of beauty. Every piece is built to be worn often, styled differently each time, and kept for years rather than a season.

Her Tomorrow is for the mahasiswi finishing her final semester, the young professional finding her voice in the boardroom, and the founder building something of her own — women who are becoming more confident, more mature, more themselves.

Made for the woman she is, and the woman she's becoming.

Care Guide +

Treat your knitwear gently to help maintain its shape and quality.

  • Gentle cold wash
  • Use a laundry bag
  • Do not wring or twist
  • Reshape while damp
  • Lay flat to dry, do not hang
  • Avoid tumble drying
  • Store folded
