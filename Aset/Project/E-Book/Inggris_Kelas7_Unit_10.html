TOPIC 1

Clothes

Clothes are things people wear on their bodies, and each type has a different function. We wear a coat when it is cold, sports clothes when we exercise, and formal clothes at special events.

To talk about clothes in English you need two things: the name of each item, and a way to say who is wearing it.

Tip

Some clothes are always plural — trousers, jeans, gloves, boots. Say a pair of trousers, not a trouser.

10.1.2 Describing clothes

We use the verb wear to talk about clothes. To say what someone has on right now, use be + wearing.

Pattern
Subject + be + wearing + clothing

Examples: She is wearing a blue dress. · He is wearing black trousers. · They are wearing jackets. — colours go before the clothing word: a red sweater · a black coat · blue jeans · white shoes.

TOPIC 2

Understanding Clothing Descriptions

When you listen to a conversation about clothes, you do not need every word. You need four things: the person's name, the type of clothing, the colour, and the situation.

Careful

Speakers often correct each other — "No, he's in a blue sweater." The second answer is the true one.

1 · LISTEN FOR KEYWORDS

"I am wearing a blue jacket."

Colour: blue · Clothing: jacket

2 · UNDERSTAND THE WHOLE DESCRIPTION

"She is wearing a black dress with high heels."

Person: she · Clothes: black dress · Shoes: high heels

TOPIC 3

Simple Present vs Present Continuous

English has two ways to talk about the present, and they mean different things. One is for what you always do; the other is for what you are doing right now.

The Big Idea

I wear a jacket in winter = a habit. I am wearing a jacket now = at this moment.

10.3.1 Simple Present

Use the Simple Present for habits and routines and for general facts.

Forms
Positive: Subject + Verb 1  →  I like clothes. · He wears a jacket. Negative: Subject + do/does not + Verb 1  →  She does not wear hats. Question: Do/Does + Subject + Verb 1?  →  Do you like this dress?

For he / she / it, add -s or -es: She likes music. · He watches TV. — Everyday examples: I usually wear a school uniform. · My father reads newspapers every morning.

10.3.2 Present Continuous

Use the Present Continuous for actions happening now, or for a temporary situation.

Forms
Positive: Subject + am/is/are + Verb-ing  →  I am wearing a jacket. Negative: Subject + am/is/are + not + Verb-ing  →  He is not wearing a coat. Question: Am/Is/Are + Subject + Verb-ing?  →  Is she wearing a dress?

Now: He is trying on a new shirt. — Temporary: She is working at a clothing store this month. — At the mall: A: What is Anna doing? B: She is looking for a new jacket.

TOPIC 4

Describing People and Clothes

Describing clothes is useful when you talk about people, shopping, or fashion. A good description has four parts: the person, the clothing, the colour, and some extra detail.

Add Detail

Colour · size · style · accessories. Finish with an opinion: She looks very elegant.

Writing Pattern
Subject + is wearing + clothes

Simple: Maria is wearing a blue sweater and black trousers. — With detail: Tom is wearing a black jacket, blue jeans, and brown boots.

Example · A Student Describes a Friend

"My friend Lisa is wearing a white shirt and a long skirt. She is also wearing black shoes. She looks very elegant."

Notice the order: person → clothes → more clothes → opinion.

Summary

Unit Summary

In this unit you learned clothing vocabulary and the grammar you need to describe what people wear.

Vocabulary

  • Clothes words: hat, sweater, boots, dress, coat, gloves, trousers, tie, scarf, high heels — and many more from the word list.
  • Colours go before the clothing word: a red sweater, blue jeans, white shoes.

Grammar

  • Simple Present — habits, routines, and facts. Form: Subject + Verb 1 (add -s/-es for he/she/it). Example: I wear a uniform every day.
  • Present Continuous — actions happening now and temporary situations. Form: Subject + am/is/are + Verb-ing. Example: She is wearing a new dress.

Listening & Writing

  • Listen for the name, the clothing, the colour, and the situation — and notice when a speaker corrects another.
  • Describe people using clothing words, colours, and the pattern Subject + is wearing + clothes.

With this unit you can describe clothes, follow conversations about fashion and shopping, and communicate better in daily situations.
